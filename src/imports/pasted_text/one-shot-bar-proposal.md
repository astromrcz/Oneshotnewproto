Project Context 

One Shot Bar and Billiards, located in San Juan, Cainta, Rizal, is a cozy recreational spot that has been welcoming customers since its establishment in 2024. Owned by Mr. Raymond Lomabas and supported by a dedicated team of four employees who maintain cleanliness, cater to walk-in customers, and monitor table rentals separated by shifts, the bar has quickly become a favorite destination for students, working adults, and families. With well-maintained billiard tables and a warm, inviting atmosphere, One Shot Bar and Billiards offer a relaxing space where guests can unwind, socialize, and enjoy friendly games throughout its 15-hour daily operation from 10 AM to 1 AM. The establishment records a maximum of 135 tables hourly rented per day reflecting its growing popularity within the community. 

 

One Shot Bar and Billiards often rely on table reservations and hourly usage to manage customer flow and maximize profitability. Despite the growing demand for structured playtime and fair access to tables, they still rely on traditional methods for time tracking and queue management. This process involves writing customer information, customer numbers, and booking table time on paper. While they have a system to track table occupancy, it lacks the functionality to monitor time effectively. Although these manual methods have proven to be reliable and effective in previous operations, the increasing number of customer walk-ins and reservations, particularly during peak hours operation from 11 AM to 6 PM, highlights the need for a more efficient and modernized approach to enhance overall service performance. The absence of a reliable system for monitoring reservations and table usage often leads to overcrowding, increasing waiting times, and challenges in monitoring tables.  

 

One Shot Bar and Billiards currently manage reservations manually and relies mainly on walk-in customers. Customers often contact staff via phone calls or group chat messages to inquire about table availability and reservation times. As the customer numbers continue to grow, particularly during peak hours, the reliance on manual processes has led to operational challenges. These key processes are outlined below:  

1. Manual Reservation Process - Customers reserve by walking in or contacting the staff, who then write the customer’s details down on paper, which can cause delays due to manual recording and retrieval of information, resulting in confusion and reducing efficiency. 

2. Unorganized Queueing System - The staff often lose track of who arrived first, causing confusion and potential unfairness during busy hours when countless customers are waiting.  

3. Inaccurate Time Tracking - Tables are monitored manually, making it difficult for staff to detect when a customer’s rented time has elapsed, resulting in possible overstays and potential disputes in which customers may contest additional charges applied to their session due to the absence of a verifiable and objective record of their rent time. 

4. On-Hold Revenue from Overtime - When customers exceed their paid time without being charged, the business loses potential income due to lack of automated timers. 

5. Peak-Hour Overcrowding - Without a formal queuing system, arrival order is not properly tracked, leading to inaccurate service and some customers being skipped. 

 

Given the significant challenges One Shot Bar and Billiards face in handling reservations, queue management, and time monitoring, there is a pressing need for a more advanced operational solution. To address these concerns, the development and implementation of an integrated management system is proposed, featuring automated reservation handling, table monitor, and queue management. This project proposal aims to modernize and enhance their current processes, reduce errors, improve efficiency, and provide better experience for both staff and customers.  

 

The rationale of this study is based on the proponent’s ability to design a system that addresses the current operational gaps in One Shot Bar and Billiards. The existing manual process requires staff to handle reservations, track table usage, monitor time, and manage queues at the same time, which increases workload and the risk of errors. 

By automating these tasks, the system reduces manual effort and ensures accurate tracking of session durations. This creates clear and reliable records of playtime, helping avoid disputes and ensuring fair billing. It also improves the overall flow of operations and reduces customer waiting time. 

 

 

Purpose and Description 

The purpose of this study is to improve how One Shot Bar and Billiards manages table reservations, customer queues, and table rentals by replacing the current paper-based process with a centralized web-based system. The proposed system combines reservation handling, real-time table monitoring, queue management, and session tracking into one platform to make daily operations more organized and easier to manage. At present, staff manually write reservations, track table usage, and manage customer queues, which often leads to mistakes, confusion, and delays during busy hours. To address these issues, the system allows staff to record both online and walk-in reservations digitally, view table availability through a live dashboard, and receive alerts when a session is about to end or has already exceeded the paid time. This helps ensure that playtime is recorded correctly and that customers are charged fairly. The system uses the First Come, First Served method for managing walk-in customers. This is supported by the study Implementation of a Website-Based Sports Facility Reservation System Using FCFS Method (2024), which found that the lack of a clear queuing system can cause scheduling conflicts and customer disputes. By applying FCFS, the system ensures that customers are served based on their arrival time, reducing the chances of customers being skipped (Implementation of a Website-Based Sports Facility Reservation System Using FCFS Method, 2024). The study also applies the concept of real-time queue monitoring from CLIQUE: A Web-Based Queue Management System with Real-Time Monitoring (2022). This study showed that displaying the queue in real time helps staff track customer order more easily and reduces confusion. In the proposed system, a queue dashboard shows the list of waiting customers in order, allowing staff to assign tables properly (CLIQUE: A Web-Based Queue Management System with Real-Time Monitoring, 2022). In addition, the study is supported by Digital Innovation and Entrepreneurial Success in the Philippine MSME Sector (2025), which explains that small businesses in the Philippines benefit from using digital systems to improve daily operations. For One Shot Bar and Billiards, using a web-based system helps reduce manual work and makes service more consistent. Overall, the system helps staff manage reservations, table usage, and queues in one place instead of relying on paper records. It reduces errors, improves how customers are handled, and keeps operations clear during busy hours. The system also generates table usage reports that show how often tables are used, helping management decide whether there is a need to add or reduce tables in the future. 

 

 

Objectives of the Study 

General Objectives 

To propose an online reservation, time-tracking, queuing, and payment processing system for One Shot Bar and Billiards to enhance business operations and customer experience. 

Equipping One Shot Bar and Billiards with a system that integrates table monitoring, reservation handling, and queue management supports accurate tracking of table usage and handles customer queues. It also enables real-time table availability, centralized data management, and analytics-driven decision-making through a web-based platform. 

Specific Objectives 

To develop an integrated table monitoring and management system that ensures accurate tracking of billiard table usage and real-time visibility of table status. 

The system utilizes automated timers to record session start and end times, eliminating manual tracking and ensuring precise billing while reducing discrepancies. A real-time display of table availability allows staff to quickly identify occupied, reserved, and available tables, improving operational efficiency and reducing customer waiting times. The system records and organizes table usage and transaction data in a structured format, establishing a reliable data foundation that supports performance evaluation and future analytical processes, while also providing automated alerts to notify staff and customers 15 minutes before a session ends, enabling timely table turnover and improving overall service efficiency. 

 

To design a centralized customer service management system that streamlines reservation handling, queue management, feedback collection, and table-related payment processing.  

The system enables staff to efficiently manage customer bookings by securely storing essential details in a centralized database, ensuring consistency, and minimizing errors. A built-in queuing mechanism organizes walk-in customers based on arrival order, promoting fairness and reducing confusion during peak hours. An integrated online payment feature supports a structured reservation process, where customers are required to pay a down payment fee set by the managers, such as 25%, upon booking to secure their table. Upon arrival, customers complete the remaining balance before accessing their reserved table. For session extensions, the system includes a dedicated feature that automatically calculates additional charges based on extended table usage. This payment functionality is limited to table rentals only and does not cover product transactions, as these remain under the existing POS system of One Shot Bar and Billiards. Furthermore, the system incorporates feedback mechanisms, such as post-service surveys linked to reservations, to capture customer insights. All collected data —from reservations, queue activity, payments, and feedback — is structured and stored to support continuous service improvement and serve as input for advanced analytics and decision-making. 

 

To propose an AI-powered analytics system that tracks table usage and supports data-driven decision-making for business expansion.  

The system will monitor real-time and historical table usage, including peak hours, occupancy rates, and customer patterns, to provide accurate insights into operational performance. By analyzing these data trends, the AI can identify periods of high demand, underutilized tables, and recurring customer behaviors. This enables management to make informed decisions on whether to add or reduce tables, optimize layout, or expand the business. Additionally, the system generates predictive insights to forecast future demand, ensuring the business can scale efficiently while maintaining smooth customer flow and maximizing profitability.
